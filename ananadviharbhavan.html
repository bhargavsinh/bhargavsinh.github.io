🏛️ Official Residence & Heritage Sanctuary

Anand Vihar Bhavan

"A sacred abode harmonizing timeless architectural heritage, Pushtimargiya spiritual devotion, and the unbroken legacy of Suryavanshi Rajputs."

🏰 Vision & Architectural Essence

Anand Vihar Bhavan stands as a living testament to dignity, heritage, and timeless values rooted deeply in the glorious lineage of Veer Shiromani Maharana Pratap Singhji and the Sisodiya Rajput tradition. Designed meticulously as a peaceful sanctuary, it reflects a balanced fusion of classical Rajput aesthetics and contemporary functional utility.

Every structural detail, arch, and open courtyard within Anand Vihar Bhavan is dedicated to upholding the principles of hospitality (Atithya), righteousness (Dharma), and self-respect, serving as a bastion of cultural continuity for generations to come.

🙏

Pushtimargiya Seva & Devotion

At the heart of Anand Vihar Bhavan lies a deeply cherished atmosphere of Gruh Seva, inspired by Shuddhadvaita philosophy and the divine teachings of the Pushtimargiya Pratharya and Mulacharyas. The daily routine resonates with pure devotion, bhajan, and Thakorji's divine grace.

📜

Library & Historical Archives

A dedicated study and archival space housing rare historical manuscripts, Suryavanshi chronicles, legal literature, and academic treatises. It serves as a productive hub for deep research, writing, and intellectual contemplation.

🌿

Tranquil & Sustainable Living

Surrounded by natural greenery and open spaces, the Bhavan provides a serene environment optimized for deep work, mental clarity, physical well-being, and peaceful family gatherings.

🛡️

Code of Honor & Hospitality

Carrying forward the traditional Rajput ethos where guests are welcomed with utmost warmth, respect, and dignity. The gates of Anand Vihar Bhavan remain open for scholars, dignitaries, and seekers of truth.

🔒

Protocol of Sacred Privacy (Nija Seva)

Important Notice: Visitors to Anand Vihar Bhavan are strictly not permitted to have Darshan or Jhanki of our Private Sevya Swaroops (Nija Mandir).
