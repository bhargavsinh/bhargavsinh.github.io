About this work

This forthcoming Hindi work examines classical ideas of rājadharma from the Mahābhārata tradition in conversation with modern democratic thought. It is framed as a sanātana discourse on justice, duty, and the foundations of the polity — “न्यायमूलं धर्मः धर्ममूलं राष्ट्रम्।”
