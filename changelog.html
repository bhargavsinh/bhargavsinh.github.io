Changelog

V18.2.1 — 2026-11-06

Blueprint V1.1 implementation (Royal Editorial × Digital Institution)

  • New design system: obsidian / ivory / royal gold, serif headings, accessible-gold text on light surfaces
  • Typography: Rasa for Gujarati, Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi, system sans-serif for English body, editorial serif for English headings; fonts load without blocking render
  • New information architecture: Home, About, Projects, Publications, Research & Heritage, Contact (hub pages), with the three portals preserved at their existing URLs
  • Home rebuilt: identity, introduction, featured projects, publications, research & heritage, institutional presence, portals, contact
  • Hub pages are generated from data/profile.json, projects.json, publications.json and research.json — every string in them is verbatim existing site text
  • Publications: searchable and filterable digital library
  • Global primary navigation plus a portal sub-bar on every portal page; breadcrumbs
  • Accessibility: contrast-checked palette, labelled landmarks, descriptive link names, visible focus, reduced-motion support
  • Performance: no render-blocking font stylesheet, 64 px and 128 px brand-mark variants with srcset, explicit image dimensions, versioned asset URLs
  • Security: strict meta CSP (no frame-ancestors), no inline scripts or styles, rel="noopener noreferrer" on every external link, security.txt
  • Sitemap lists canonical URLs only, with real lastmod values
  • Service worker: network-first pages, update prompt, stale cache purge on activate
  • Fixed: pwriter output used literal "\n" and mis-counted words; pushtidoctor.html redirect target
  • All earlier URLs keep working (legacy root redirects retained)
