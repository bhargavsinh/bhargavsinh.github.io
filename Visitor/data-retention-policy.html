Data Retention & Deletion

This static site does not operate its own user database. Personal data mainly arises when you submit the contact / appointment form.

What is retained

  • Form fields you submit (name, email, phone, preferred time, message, etc.)
  • Delivery metadata held by the form processor (Formspree) and the author’s email inbox

Retention period

Form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and manage appointments—typically up to 24 months, unless a longer period is required for an ongoing matter or legal obligation. You may request earlier deletion.

Deletion requests

Email bhargavsinh.bca@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and enough detail to locate your submission. We will confirm when records under our control have been removed, subject to legal holds.

Browser storage

Intro flags and similar values live only in your browser; clear site data in your browser settings to remove them.
