Cookie & Tracking Policy

This site is hosted as a static website on GitHub Pages. It does not run advertising trackers or sell personal data.

What we use

  • Essential local/session storage: intro “seen” flags, last portal preference, or form rate-limit timestamps in your browser only.
  • Service worker cache: offline/app-shell assets for performance.
  • Third-party fonts: Google Fonts may set technical cookies under Google’s policies.
  • Form provider: Formspree logs follow Formspree’s policy when you submit the contact form.

What we do not use by default

  • Advertising networks or marketing pixels
  • Google Analytics (unless later added and disclosed here)

Consent

Because this site does not set non-essential advertising cookies by default, a marketing cookie banner is not shown.
