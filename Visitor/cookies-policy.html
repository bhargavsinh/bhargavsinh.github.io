Cookie & Tracking Policy

This site is hosted as a static website on GitHub Pages. It does not run advertising trackers or sell personal data.

What we use

  • Essential local/session storage: may store intro “seen” flags, last portal preference, or form rate-limit timestamps in your browser only. These are not sent to our server as analytics events.
  • Service worker cache: offline/app-shell assets may be stored by the browser for performance and offline fallback.
  • Third-party fonts: Google Fonts may set technical cookies or connection logs under Google’s policies when those resources load.
  • Form provider: submissions go to Formspree; their cookies/logs follow Formspree’s policy when you submit the contact form.

What we do not use by default

  • Advertising networks or marketing pixels
  • Google Analytics (unless later added and disclosed here)
  • Cross-site behavioural advertising profiles

Consent

Because this site does not set non-essential advertising cookies by default, a marketing cookie banner is not shown. If analytics or non-essential cookies are added later, this policy and any consent UI will be updated first.
