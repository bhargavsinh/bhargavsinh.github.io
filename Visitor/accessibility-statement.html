Accessibility Statement

BHARGAVSINH SISODIYA aims to make this portfolio usable for as many people as possible.

Measures in place

  • Semantic HTML and labelled controls where implemented
  • Keyboard access to primary navigation; skip/dismiss for intro
  • Visible focus styles in portal chrome
  • Respect for prefers-reduced-motion
  • Dark theme with high-contrast red/white on black

Known limitations

  • Some legacy pages use Tailwind CDN and may need contrast review.
  • Complex genealogy embeds may not fully expose screen-reader semantics.
