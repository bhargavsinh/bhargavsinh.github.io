Accessibility Statement

BHARGAVSINH SISODIYA aims to make this portfolio usable for as many people as possible.

Measures in place

  • Semantic HTML structure and labelled controls where implemented
  • Keyboard access to primary navigation and skip/dismiss for the cinematic intro
  • Visible focus styles in portal chrome
  • Respect for prefers-reduced-motion on the intro animation
  • Dark theme with high-contrast red/white accents on black

Known limitations

  • Some legacy pages still rely on third-party CSS (e.g. Tailwind CDN) and may need further contrast review.
  • Complex genealogy embeds may not expose full screen-reader semantics.

Feedback

Report accessibility barriers: bhargavsinh.bca@gmail.com or the contact form. Please include the page URL and a short description.
