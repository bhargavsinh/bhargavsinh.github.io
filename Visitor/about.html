About Bhargavsinh Sisodiya

I am Bhargavsinh Mahendrasinh Sisodiya, a Bachelor of Computer Applications student, Gujarati author, aspiring web developer and independent digital creator from Gujarat, India.

My journey brings together modern computing, literature, Pushtimargiya devotion, family heritage and digital preservation. I am interested in using technology to organize knowledge, communicate ideas and help preserve meaningful cultural and literary resources.

My guiding direction

To connect the discipline of code, the responsibility of knowledge, the dignity of heritage and the spirit of sincere seva.

02. Academic Journey

My degree provides a foundation in programming, information systems, databases, networking and the practical use of computers. I aim to strengthen this foundation through independent projects and continuous technical learning.

Areas of academic study

  • Computer Networking: communication between systems and the fundamentals of networked computing.

  • E-Commerce: digital business models and online commercial systems.
  • English and Indian Knowledge Systems: communication, cultural learning and broader intellectual perspectives.

I believe practical learning is essential. Writing code, identifying errors, testing solutions and explaining a concept in simple language are valuable parts of understanding a technical subject.

05. Pushtimargiya Vaishnav Tradition

I am devoted to the Pushti Bhakti Marg, the devotional tradition associated with Shri Vallabhacharyaji, revered as Shri Mahaprabhuji. Its philosophical foundation includes Shuddhadvaita Vedanta and the central importance of divine grace in the devotional path.

My interest extends to Pushtimargiya philosophy, literature, devotional practices and historical development. I regard the tradition as a living spiritual inheritance that deserves thoughtful study and faithful preservation.

Areas of study and interest

  • Shuddhadvaita Vedanta and Pushtimargiya Siddhanta.
  • The teachings and writings associated with Shri Mahaprabhuji and Shri Gusainji.
  • The Sodash Granth and other Pushtimargiya texts.
  • Grih Seva, Nitya Seva and devotional life in the home.
  • The relationship between Siddhanta, Seva and devotional understanding.
  • The preservation, transcription and digital organization of traditional literature.
  • Accurate textual attribution and reliable source references.
My devotional orientation

My devotional life centres on Shrinathji. I seek to deepen my understanding of Pushtimarg through study, reflection, seva and respect for the tradition's established teachings.

I believe religious and historical writing requires particular care. An original textual passage, a traditional interpretation, a later commentary and a personal reflection should not be presented as though they were all the same.

06. Shri Devdamanprabhu & Seva

Shrinathji occupies a central place in my devotional world. My interest in Seva also inspires practical questions about organizing devotional schedules, reference materials, personal notes and records of important occasions.

I explore how digital tools can support organization without replacing the devotional meaning, personal responsibility or traditional guidance associated with Seva.

SDSP — Shri Devdamanlal Seva Planner

SDSP is a project concept intended to help organize information relevant to Seva dedicated to Shri Devdamanprabhu. Its design direction emphasizes personal organization, privacy and ease of use.

  • Planning and recording daily Seva routines.
  • Organizing special Utsavs and Manoraths.
  • Maintaining notes, photographs and reference documents.
  • Calendar-based planning and historical records.
  • Reminders and structured devotional information.
  • Offline-friendly functionality where feasible.
  • Manual Vikram Samvat date organization where required.

These points describe the project's intended direction, not a guarantee that every feature is currently implemented or publicly available.

08. Bhargav Lipi & Symbolic Systems

Bhargav Lipi is a personal creative and intellectual project related to symbolic writing systems. I am interested in the relationship between visual symbols, consistent rules, written representation and digital implementation.

Developing a script or symbolic system can involve documenting individual symbols, establishing conventions, organizing mappings and considering how the system could be represented through digital tools.

My interest in Bhargav Lipi reflects a desire to combine creativity with systematic thinking. Visual expression and technical structure can work together when a project is documented carefully.

Project status and historical clarity

Bhargav Lipi is presented here as a personal project. It should not be confused with an established ancient script or historically authenticated writing system without independent supporting evidence.

09. Family Heritage & Mewar

My family identity and interest in historical heritage form an important part of my personal story. I have a particular interest in Mewar and the historical legacy associated with Shri Maharana Pratap Singhji.

Shri Maharana Pratap Singhji is remembered in Indian history for his resistance, courage and role in Mewar's history. My interest in this heritage motivates me to learn about genealogy, historical context and the preservation of family records.

Our family genealogy

According to my family's stated genealogy, I trace my ancestry through Shri Puranmalji, described in our family tradition as a son of Shri Maharana Pratap Singhji. Our family record presents a sixteen-generation line leading to me.

I believe inherited history deserves both dignity and careful documentation. Family tradition, oral accounts, archival documents and independently established historical facts should be identified clearly rather than conflated.
