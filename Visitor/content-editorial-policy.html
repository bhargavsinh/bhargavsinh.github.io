Content & Editorial Policy

This portfolio publishes personal writing, Gujarati literature, historical notes, genealogy and technical work by BHARGAVSINH SISODIYA.

Editorial standards

  • Research and historical pages should cite sources where practical.
  • Genealogy may combine documentary records and family tradition; uncertain links should be labelled as such.
  • Religious or Pushtimarg-related writing reflects the author’s understanding and is not a substitute for guidance from qualified teachers or institutions.
  • Opinion and interpretation are distinguished from primary quotations when possible.

Corrections

Factual errors can be reported via the contact form or email. Material corrections may be noted with an updated date on the page.

No professional advice

Content is educational and informational. It is not legal, medical, financial or spiritual professional advice.
